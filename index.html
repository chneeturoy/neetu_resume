About This Guide

This platform simplifies project management into practical steps that help organisations plan, execute, and deliver successful outcomes. It combines academic knowledge with real-world application tailored for small businesses and charities in Ireland.

Core Project Management Areas

Planning

Define goals (SMART), break work into tasks, and create timelines.

Execution

Monitor progress, communicate effectively, and manage tasks.

Risk

Identify risks early and plan mitigation strategies.

Teams

Build strong teams with clear roles and leadership.

Stakeholders

Engage stakeholders through clear communication and updates.

Methodologies

Use Agile, Waterfall, or Hybrid approaches based on your needs.
